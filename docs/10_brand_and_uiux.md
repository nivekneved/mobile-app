## UI/UX Guidelines
- **Layouts**: Primary conversion forms (Booking Wizard, Tailor-made inquiries) must use full-page immersive layouts to prevent inner scrolling issues.
- **Occupancy Fields**: Expose Adults, Teens (13-17), Children (2-12), and Infants (0-2) explicitly as standard inputs.
- **Grid Layout**: Display meal plans in a single, balanced horizontal row on desktop.
